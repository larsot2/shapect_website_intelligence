Guide

How much does a bathroom renovation cost on the Northern Beaches?

Most of our full bathroom renovations fall between $25,000 and $45,000, depending on size, layout changes and fixtures.

Moving plumbing, enlarging a shower or choosing premium tiles all move the number. Keeping the existing layout is the biggest single saving.

Why we don't quote online

We don't offer online quotes, because every bathroom is different. Instead we visit, measure and give you a fixed-price written quote.

Common questions

Do you do small jobs? We don't take on partial makeovers such as re-grouting or vanity swaps only.

How long does it take? A typical full bathroom takes three to four weeks on site.
